# HackList usage guidelines

These rules are provisional until the owner approves the source and final master geometry.

## Approved for review

- Preserve the supplied compass proportions and central `H`.
- Use cream, paper, forest, olive, signal, and gold as distinct roles.
- Use the compass to communicate navigation, discovery, or verification.
- Keep evidence metadata calmer and denser than editorial display copy.
- Prefer a stable mark with motion around it. The compass does not spin.

## Do not do yet

- Do not redraw or vectorize the mark.
- Do not create a compact monogram or wordmark lockup.
- Do not stretch, rotate, outline, shadow, or recolor the mark.
- Do not publish a social, print, presentation, or partner template.
- Do not treat provisional colors as final print values.
